# Cheese Story Shot Briefs (Starter Set)

These are starter prompts for scene generation and storyboarding.

## Shot A — Jacques solo
- Character: Jacques only
- Action: Jacques presents a covered silver platter with a confident flourish.
- Framing: Slightly low camera angle; top of his top hat cropped out of frame.
- Setting: Warm elegant restaurant interior with golden ambient light.
- Style notes: Playful expression, clear silhouette, quick-read composition.
- Exclusion rule: Marco and Gus are not present.

## Shot B — Marco solo
- Character: Marco only
- Action: Marco balances a tower of cheese wedges and grins at the camera.
- Framing: Marco fits comfortably inside the frame with clean headroom.
- Setting: Warm elegant restaurant interior with polished wood and soft highlights.
- Style notes: Round, cheerful shape language; bold readable pose.
- Exclusion rule: Jacques and Gus are not present.

## Shot C — Gus solo
- Character: Gus only
- Action: Gus proudly rings a small service bell beside a tiny cheese cart.
- Framing: Slightly higher camera angle with visible space above his head.
- Setting: Warm elegant restaurant interior with cozy depth and rich color.
- Style notes: Compact, energetic pose; playful and instantly readable.
- Exclusion rule: Jacques and Marco are not present.
